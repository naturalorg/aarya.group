Buyer’s guide

Know exactly what you are buying

A good purchase is a well-documented one. Our team checks the following on every property.

    A-Khata vs B-Khata

    A-Khata properties are fully approved and eligible for bank loans and building plan sanction. B-Khata properties often have approval gaps and can limit financing options.

    BDA, BMRDA and DTCP

    These are the planning authorities that approve layouts in and around Bengaluru. We confirm which authority covers your site and that the layout approval is valid.

    Land use and conversion

    Agricultural land must be converted for residential or commercial use. We check the conversion order before any advance is paid.
